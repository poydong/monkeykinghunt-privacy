Privacy Policy for MonkeyKingHunt

Effective Date: 2026-05-16

1. Introduction

MonkeyKingHunt ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data.

2. When Data is Collected

No data is collected automatically when the app is launched. All data collection (including anonymous analytics and advertising ID) occurs only after you explicitly agree to this policy and interact with the app. No network requests, ad loading, or data collection happen before user consent.

3. Data We Collect (Only with Consent)

We only collect non-personal, anonymous data:

  • Advertising ID (AAID/GAID): Used solely for displaying rewarded and interstitial ads.
  • Anonymous Device Information: Such as OS version and device model, for SDK stability.
  • Anonymous Game Analytics: Such as level completion, for improving game design.

We do NOT collect: IMEI, MAC address, location data, contacts, photos, or any personally identifiable information.

4. How We Use Data

  • To display ads that you choose to view (e.g., rewarded videos for in-game rewards).
  • To analyze anonymous game usage to improve the app.
  • To maintain the stability of third-party SDKs.

5. Third-Party SDKs & Advertising Disclosure

The App includes advertisements: rewarded ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads.

Ads are provided by third-party advertising SDKs, which may use anonymous Advertising ID to provide personalized advertising.

You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.

The App uses the following third-party SDKs:

  • XYSDK: For advertising and analytics. All network calls are user-initiated.
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements.

6. Your Rights

You may opt out of interest-based ads through your device settings.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
